How to deliver

1. Stop inside the glowing ring at a milk shop (🛒) and press LOAD MILK — you carry up to 6 bottles.

2. Drive to a house with a pink ring (🏠) and press DELIVER.

3. Press 🚪 to step out. On foot you get your own pad — ⬆ ⬅ ➡ ⬇ to walk, 🏃 RUN to jog and 🤸 JUMP to hop. Press DELIVER and the courier carries the bottle to the door, hands it over, walks back and climbs into the truck by themselves. Cars and trees cannot be driven through — a hard crash spills a bottle.

4. The world shifts by itself: farm → ocean → fire. Want a different view? Tap the 🎨 button (or press T) to lock the theme by hand — farm → ocean → fire → auto.
