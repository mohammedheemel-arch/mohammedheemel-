What is freshmilkfarmabdur?

Fresh Milk Farm Abdur is the home of Nosto Alchemist — a cosy, hand-drawn brewing game and a full 3D bazaar brawler that runs in any browser. Phone, tablet or PC; nothing to install, and free to play.

In the 3D bazaar you pick your fighter from six characters — the Alchemist, Ani Das, Abdur Rohaman, Nusrat, Argo Dev and Mohammed Heemel. Whoever you leave behind becomes your rival, their stall becomes yours, and their signature skill joins your eight. Settle five champion stalls, pick from ten guns in the armory, wander an 116 × 116 metre market full of cows, dogs, cats and chickens, and change the whole world between the jungle, the undersea reef and the ember wastes. The interface speaks English and বাংলা.
