Nosto Alchemist — the free 3D bazaar game at freshmilkfarmabdur.com

freshmilkfarmabdur is the home of Nosto Alchemist: a cosy, hand-painted brewing story and a full 3D bazaar brawler that runs in any browser — on a phone, a tablet or a PC, with nothing to install.

In the bazaar you choose your fighter from six characters (the Alchemist, Ani Das, Abdur Rohaman, Nusrat, Argo Dev and Mohammed Heemel). Whoever you leave behind becomes your rival, their stall becomes yours, and their signature skill becomes one of your twelve skills. You settle five champion stalls, ten guns, meet the cows, dogs, cats and chickens of the market, and switch the whole world through six worlds that change automatically.
